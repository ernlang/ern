# ERN — Concurrency Model Specification v0
## WC3.1 — Targeted Corrections

**Status:** Non-normative design candidate — pending validation and exploratory formalization.

**Basis:** Working Candidate 3 and formalization report `spec2-m1-wc3.md`.

These corrections replace only the corresponding provisions of WC3. All unaffected provisions remain unchanged.

### C13 — `newMutex` Construction

Add a construction precondition:

Every structural leaf in `footprint(M)` MUST be `Init` before `newMutex` succeeds. Construction does not implicitly initialize these leaves.

Storage merely owned by `M`, but not structurally included in `footprint(M)`, is not subject to this requirement solely by virtue of ownership.

### C21 — `SyncInv`

Add an initialization invariant:

For every live synchronization entry, all structural leaves of its `guarded`-holding field `slot`, and its associated controller leaf `ctl`, MUST remain `Init`.

Preservation of this property by every permitted transition is a proof obligation, not a transition precondition that may be assumed without justification.

### C22.3 — Encapsulation of Transfers

Replace the transfer restriction with:

While a synchronization entry is live, the safe mutex abstraction MUST prevent an independent `transfer` whose source is:

1. The field `slot` holding `guarded`;
2. Any strict structural subplace of `slot`;
3. The associated controller leaf `ctl`.

A transfer of the complete mutex value `M`, or of a structurally enclosing value, remains eligible for O4 under its own unchanged preconditions.

Preservation of `SyncInv`, including controller uniqueness, controller association, and initialization, by such transfers is a proof obligation.

This restriction belongs to the safe mutex abstraction. It does not modify the ordinary authorization rules or outcomes of O4.

### C15.3 — Authorization of `deriveSync`

Clarify:

`deriveSync` is authorized exclusively through `K_C.SyncAuthorized`.

Neither ordinary holder-based alternative of `ExercisableAt` independently authorizes `deriveSync`.

Ordinary derivation through O1 remains available under its unchanged preconditions.

The source activity, own-mode, origin-chain, effective-mode, and exclusivity requirements specified elsewhere in C15 remain unchanged.

### C18.3 — `resetLock` Controller Validity

Clarify:

The associated `ControlOf(entry)` MUST resolve to an existing usable controller leaf satisfying the controller-validity requirements of C21.

This condition does not replace the separate recovery-authority and active-descendant requirements.

### C5.3 — Value Transmission and Parameter Yielding

Clarify:

At the beginning of the parameter-yield phase, every transmitted authority MUST satisfy the active, derived, and creator-frame-held requirements corresponding to Spec 1 §72.1, evaluated in the state produced by the preceding value transfers.

All other conditions of the composite by-value `spawn` remain unchanged.

### Interface and Realization Obligations

The following provisions remain explicit interface or realization obligations, rather than properties claimed to be established by the abstract authority state `S_C` alone:

- C13.3: Non-falsifiability through the safe abstraction interface.
- C13.6: Enforcement of the controller synchronization protocol.
- C13.10: Correspondence with the initial physical controller state.
- C22.4: Exclusion of unauthorized ordinary writes to the physical controller.
- C17 and C18: Concrete synchronization-state updates and their atomic realization.

### Formalization Coverage

The combined trace model MUST include the by-value `spawn` transition in `LStep`.

The corrective verification must establish preservation of the complete `SyncInv` and `GrantDiscipline` for every transition admitted by the encapsulation contract, including `transfer` and by-value `spawn`.

In particular, the initialization invariant must be checked against every applicable inherited kernel operation O1–O16 and every new candidate transition.

If a permitted operation violates the invariant, the formalization MUST report a concrete counterexample, identify the enabling rule, and leave the corresponding corrective design decision open. No additional encapsulation restriction may be introduced silently.

A proof restricted to traces without `transfer`, without by-value `spawn`, or to a weakened synchronization invariant does not discharge this obligation.

---

**End of WC3.1 — Targeted Corrections.**

**No normative stabilization is proposed.**
